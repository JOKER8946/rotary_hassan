July 2026 Programme

Seven events were conducted in July 2026 — reported in full in the July edition of our club bulletin, MYTHRI.

01
Jul

RO Water Purifier Donated to Karnataka State Open University

A purifier worth ₹35,000 sponsored by Rtn. Pani Kumar B, installed at KSOU, SMK Nagar, Arsikere Road. Doctors' Day, Chartered Accountants' Day and GST Day were also observed, followed by a breakfast meeting.

Service Project
02
Jul

Weekly Meeting — Installation Preparations

Chiranthana office, K. R. Puram. Programme flow, backstage coordination, responsibilities and logistics finalised for the Installation Ceremony; Rtn. PAG Pradeep entrusted with the Master of Ceremony role.

Club Meeting
09
Jul

Installation Ceremony — Team Aikyam 2026–27

Nandagokula Convention Centre, Hassan. Rtn. Dr. Geetha Kiran A installed as President — the club's first lady president — with the Board and newly inducted members, by Installation Officer PDG Rtn. Keshav H. R.

Club Milestone
16
Jul

Classification Talks by New Members

Chiranthana, K. R. Puram. New members shared their professional journeys; addressed by PDG Rtn. M. K. G. Kumar and IPDG Rtn. K. Palaksha.

New Members
23
Jul

Orientation Programme for New Members

Chiranthana, Hassan. Sessions by PDG Rtn. B. N. Ramesh, IPDG Rtn. K. Palaksha and PAG Rtn. Pradeep on Rotary's history, its structure, and the responsibilities of office bearers.

New Members
24
Jul

Installation of the Rotaract Club of Malnad College of Engineering

Alumni Hall, MCE, Hassan. Our President served as Installation Officer, installing Rtr. Suraj C. S. and his team; graced by District Governor Rtn. B. M. Bhat.

Youth Service
30
Jul

Expert Session — “Sukha–Dukha” by Dr. H. L. Mallesh Gowda

Rotary Suvarna Bhavan, Hassan. A session on balancing happiness and sorrow. The Team VISTARA 2026 committee also invited members to the district seminar on 29–30 August 2026.
