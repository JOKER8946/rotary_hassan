About Rotary Club Hassan

Empowering Hassan district for over four decades through community health, education, youth leadership, and humanitarian service under Rotary International District 3182.

Our Legacy & Motto

Together, We Serve, Inspire & Transform

Rotary Club Hassan (RI Club No. 15716, District 3182, Zone 9) unites business leaders, doctors, academics, engineers, and civic leaders in service to Hassan district.

Team Aikyam 2026–27 Theme

Led by President Rtn. Dr. Geetha Kiran A — the first lady president in the club's history — and Secretary Rtn. Sunitha Somashekar, Team Aikyam embodies the Kannada concept of Aikyam (Unity). We bring together diverse professionals to make a tangible, long-term impact on community health, education, literacy, and civic infrastructure.

The team was installed on 9 July 2026 at Nandagokula Convention Centre by Installation Officer PDG Rtn. Keshav H. R., and opened the year with a service project on 1 July — an RO water purifier for Karnataka State Open University, Hassan. From adopting rural schools under the Happy School initiative to organizing mega blood donation drives and building public commuter shelters, Rotary Club Hassan continues its proud legacy of Service Above Self.
