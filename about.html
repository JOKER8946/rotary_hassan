History of the Rotary Club of Hassan

A legacy of service, fellowship and leadership — built over several decades on Rotary's enduring principle of Service Above Self.

Our Journey

An integral part of the Rotary movement in Hassan

The Rotary Club of Hassan is the senior-most Rotary Club in Rotary International District 3182 and has been an integral part of the Rotary movement in Hassan for several decades. Built on Rotary's enduring principle of “Service Above Self,” the Club has brought together professionals, entrepreneurs, community leaders and service-minded individuals with a shared commitment to improving lives and creating lasting positive change.

Over the decades, the Club has evolved into a strong institution of service, fellowship and leadership, while remaining deeply connected to the needs and aspirations of the Hassan community. From its formative years, it has played an important role in promoting the ideals of Rotary and strengthening the culture of voluntary service in the region.

Generation after generation of Rotarians has contributed time, professional expertise, leadership and resources toward addressing important societal needs. The Club's initiatives have reached people across different sections of society and have reflected Rotary's commitment to sustainable community development.

A Distinguished Legacy of Rotary Leadership

Six District Governors across five decades

One of the proudest chapters in the history of the Rotary Club of Hassan is its contribution to Rotary leadership at the District level — a strong tradition of nurturing leaders capable of serving Rotary beyond the Club and the Hassan community.

KA
District Governor · 1975–77

Late PDG Rtn. K. Ananthramaiah

Late PDG Rtn. K. Ananthramaiah was among the pioneering leaders of the Rotary Club of Hassan to take up the responsibility of District Governor. His leadership during 1975–77 represents an important early milestone in the Club's history and laid a strong foundation for the generations of Rotary leaders who followed.

PB
District Governor · 1982–83

Late PDG Rtn. P. Y. Bushan

Late PDG Rtn. P. Y. Bushan carried forward the Club's growing tradition of Rotary leadership and served as District Governor during 1982–83. His tenure added another significant chapter to the Club's contribution to Rotary at the District level and strengthened its standing within the wider Rotary movement.

GK
District Governor · 1993–94

PDG Rtn. M. K. Goverdhan Kumar

PDG Rtn. M. K. Goverdhan Kumar served as District Governor during 1993–94, continuing the distinguished leadership journey of the Rotary Club of Hassan. Through his leadership and continued association with Rotary, he has contributed to strengthening the values of fellowship, service and institutional development that remain central to the Club.

SN
District Governor · 2007–08

Late PDG Rtn. S. K. Nagendran

Late PDG Rtn. S. K. Nagendran served as District Governor during 2007–08, carrying forward the Club's proud tradition of contributing experienced and committed Rotarians to District leadership. His service forms an important part of the Club's leadership heritage and continues to be remembered as part of its distinguished history.

BR
District Governor · 2019–20

PDG Rtn. B. N. Ramesh

PDG Rtn. B. N. Ramesh has made significant contributions to the growth and development of the Rotary Club of Hassan. He served as President of the Club during 1990–91 and subsequently took up several important responsibilities within Rotary before serving as District Governor during 2019–20.

His contribution also extends to strengthening Rotary's institutional infrastructure in Hassan. As Chairman of Rotary Trust, Hassan, he was instrumental in the establishment of Rotary Suvarna Bhavan in 2006, an important milestone in the Club's journey.

KP
District Governor · 2025–26

IPDG Rtn. K. Palaksha

IPDG Rtn. K. Palaksha represents the latest chapter in the Rotary Club of Hassan's distinguished District leadership legacy. He served as Secretary of the Club during 2003–04 and as President during 2004–05, and subsequently shouldered several responsibilities at the District level before serving as District Governor of Rotary International District 3182 during 2025–26.

His journey from Club leadership to District Governor reflects the Club's continuing tradition of nurturing committed leaders and contributing to the growth of Rotary across the District.

Their collective contribution is a source of immense pride to the Rotary Club of Hassan and stands as testimony to the Club's enduring commitment to developing leaders who serve Rotary and society with dedication.

2006
A Milestone in Our Journey

Rotary Suvarna Bhavan

A significant milestone in the institutional journey of Rotary in Hassan was the establishment of Rotary Suvarna Bhavan in 2006. With the instrumental involvement of PDG Rtn. B. N. Ramesh, as Chairman of Rotary Trust, Hassan, it emerged as an important centre for Rotary fellowship and service activities.

More than a meeting venue, Rotary Suvarna Bhavan represents the collective vision, commitment and fellowship of Rotarians in Hassan. Over the years, it has provided a platform for meetings, planning, fellowship, community programmes and various Rotary initiatives. It stands today as a symbol of Rotary's enduring presence in Hassan.

What Holds The Club Together

Service, youth and fellowship

🤝

Service to the Community

Community service has always remained at the heart of the Rotary Club of Hassan. The Club has continually responded to the changing needs of society through initiatives in healthcare, education, sanitation, environment, community welfare and social awareness.

Its activities have included healthcare and eye-care programmes, support to schools and students, environmental initiatives, public-awareness programmes, community infrastructure, health and hygiene initiatives and assistance to communities in need — seeking not merely to undertake individual projects, but to create meaningful and sustainable impact.

🎓

Empowering Youth and Future Leaders

The Club strongly believes that investing in young people is an investment in the future. Through its association with Rotaract, educational institutions and youth-oriented programmes, it encourages students and young professionals to develop leadership, professional skills, social responsibility and a spirit of volunteerism.

By creating opportunities for interaction between experienced Rotarians and young people, the Club helps bridge generations and inspires emerging leaders to contribute meaningfully to society.

💛

Fellowship — The Strength Behind Service

At the heart of the Rotary Club of Hassan is the spirit of fellowship. The Club provides a platform where individuals from diverse professions and backgrounds come together with a common purpose.

The friendships, professional connections and shared values developed through Rotary have strengthened the Club and enabled members to collectively undertake initiatives that create a greater impact. This combination of fellowship and service has remained one of the Club's greatest strengths throughout its journey.

Honouring the Past, Serving the Present, Building the Future

The story of the Rotarians who built it

The contributions of its past leaders, members and six District Governors have created a strong foundation upon which the Club continues to grow. While the needs of society and the nature of service continue to evolve, the fundamental purpose of the Rotary Club of Hassan remains unchanged — to bring people together, identify community needs, develop leaders and create positive and sustainable change through service.

Today, the Club proudly carries forward its rich heritage while embracing new ideas, partnerships, technology and opportunities to serve. With a distinguished past, an active present and a vision for an impactful future, it continues its journey of fellowship, leadership and service to humanity.
