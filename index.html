Team Aikyam
Together · Serve · Inspire · Transform

Service above self, right here in Hassan.

We're a community of professionals, academics, doctors, engineers, and entrepreneurs serving Hassan district — from blood donation camps to youth mentorship. This year, Team Aikyam carries that forward under the banner "Together · Serve · Inspire · Transform."

"We make a living by what we get, but we make a life by what we give."

Month in Review · July 2026

How Team Aikyam started the year

The first month of the 2026–27 Rotary year brought seven events — a service project on day one, the Installation Ceremony, two programmes for our newly inducted members, a Rotaract installation, and an expert session.

9 July 2026

Installation Ceremony

Rtn. Dr. Geetha Kiran A took office as the club's first lady president at Nandagokula Convention Centre, installed by PDG Rtn. Keshav H. R.

See the July record
1 July 2026

Safe Drinking Water for KSOU

An RO water purifier worth ₹35,000 donated to Karnataka State Open University, Hassan — sponsored by Rtn. Pani Kumar B.

See the July record
24 July 2026

Rotaract MCE Installation

Our President served as Installation Officer for the Rotaract Club of Malnad College of Engineering, installing Rtr. Suraj C. S. and his team.
